# Cloudiax AG

> Cloudiax AG is a managed private cloud provider, specializing in SAP Business One
> cloud hosting, AI server infrastructure, ERP application hosting, and digital
> workflow solutions. Headquartered in Rees, Germany, with data centers in Germany,
> Canada, and Singapore. Serving 20000+ cloud users through 290+ partners in 90+
> countries. ISO 27001 certified. 99.5% uptime SLA.

## Core Products

- [SAP Business One Private Cloud](https://www.cloudiax.com/sap-business-one-cloud/): Managed SAP B1 cloud hosting, on HANA or SQL, data centers in DE/CA/SG.
- [AI Managed LLM](https://www.cloudiax.com/ai-managed-llm/): Private, managed Llama 3.1-8B and 3.3-70B inference servers hosted in Germany, REST API + Chat GUI, 99.5% SLA.
- [AI Private Server](https://www.cloudiax.com/ai-private-server/): Dedicated GPU server for AI workloads, TIER 3 German data center.
- [AXeLANT](https://www.cloudiax.com/axelant/): Intelligent AI agents for SAP Business One.
- [Accantum DMS](https://www.cloudiax.com/accantum/): Digital document and workflow management for SAP Business One.
- [Accantum WMS](https://www.cloudiax.com/accantum-wms/): Warehouse management for SAP Business One.
- [SAP Customer Checkout (POS)](https://www.cloudiax.com/sap-cco/): Cloud-based point-of-sale for SAP Business One.
- [Cloud Infrastructure](https://www.cloudiax.com/datacenter/): Application servers, Kubernetes, 200 Gbit/s peering.
- [Software2Cloud](https://www.cloudiax.com/software-to-cloud/): Migration of on-premise software to Cloudiax cloud.
